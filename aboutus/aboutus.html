Our Mission

To promote and provide year-round sports training and athletic competition in a variety of Olympic-type sports for children and adults with intellectual disabilities, giving them continuing opportunities to develop physical fitness, demonstrate courage, experience joy, and participate in a sharing of gifts, skills, and friendship with their families, other Special Olympics athletes, and the community.

Our Journey in Singapore

Core Objectives

🏆
Organise continuous sports training programmes and competitions for children and adults with intellectual disabilities.
🌍
Provide opportunities for athletes to represent and participate in National, Regional, and World Special Olympics Games.
🤝
Promote social integration between Special Olympics athletes and mainstream athletes through unified team sports.
❤️
Encourage family involvement, strengthen community ties, and increase public awareness and respect for individuals with intellectual disabilities.
