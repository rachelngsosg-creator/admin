Who We Are

About Special Olympics Singapore

Four Decades of Empowerment

Established in April 1983 as an adjunct committee of MINDS and APSN, SOSG was registered as an independent voluntary welfare organisation on 15 January 1991 and as a charity on 22 February 1994.

We are a certified Institution of a Public Character (IPC status valid through 15 September 2027) and affiliated with NCSS, SNOC, Sport Singapore, and Special Olympics International.

Key Registration Details

  • UEN: S91SS0003B

Sports & Key Programmes

Core Sport

Athletics

Year-round training in track and field events, fostering speed, strength, and endurance.

Core Sport

Badminton

Fostering racquet skills, agility, and hand-eye coordination through singles and doubles play.

Core Sport

Basketball

Building teamwork, ball-handling, and strategic court play in an energetic team environment.

Core Sport

Bocce

A precision ball sport emphasizing control, strategy, and inclusive participation across all skill levels.

Core Sport

Bowling

Targeting focus and technique in regular league play and competitive bowling events.

Core Sport

DanceSport

Combines rhythm, movement, and expressive dance performance for fitness and social connection.

Core Sport

Floorball

A fast-paced indoor hockey-style sport promoting quick reflexes and team strategy.

Core Sport

Football

Developing fundamental soccer skills, tactical cooperation, and camaraderie on the field.

Core Sport

Swimming

Comprehensive aquatics training building water safety, stamina, and competitive stroke techniques.

Flagship Programme

Athlete Leadership (ALP)

Empowers athletes with digital literacy (Canva, Google Tools) alongside NUS Youth Leaders, public speaking, and advocacy skills.

Flagship Programme

Young Athletes Programme

Structured movement and social skill development for children aged 2–7 with and without intellectual disabilities.

Flagship Programme

Healthy Athletes Programme

Free health screenings including MedFest, Special Smiles, Opening Eyes, Fit Feet, and FUNfitness.

Flagship Programme

Family Support Network (FSN)

"Family Supports Family" initiative fostering bonding outings, community interaction, and mutual support.

Flagship Programme

Motor Activity Training (MATP)

Tailored motor skill activities designed for athletes with severe physical or intellectual challenges.

Frequently Asked Questions

Individuals with intellectual disabilities can join through our affiliate partner schools (MINDS, APSN) or by directly contacting the SOSG administration office.

We welcome volunteers for sports coaching, event coordination, and administrative support. In FY2025/2026, over 2,044 volunteers supported our activities.

Play Inclusive is Singapore's largest unified sports competition where athletes with and without intellectual disabilities train and compete together in partnership with Sport Singapore.
