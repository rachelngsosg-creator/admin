Our Mission

To promote and provide year-round sports training and athletic competition in a variety of Olympic-type sports for children and adults with intellectual disabilities, giving them continuing opportunities to develop physical fitness, demonstrate courage, experience joy, and participate in a sharing of gifts, skills, and friendship with their families, other Special Olympics athletes, and the community.

Our Journey in Singapore

April 1983
Founding
Established in Singapore as an adjunct committee of MINDS and APSN (formerly Association for Educationally Sub-Normal Children).
15 January 1991
VWO Registration
Officially registered as an independent voluntary welfare organisation with the Registrar of Societies.
22 February 1994
Charity Status
Officially registered as a charity with the Commissioner of Charities (IPC status valid through 15 Sept 2027).

Core Objectives

🏆
Organise continuous sports training programmes and competitions for children and adults with intellectual disabilities.
🌍
Provide opportunities for athletes to represent and participate in National, Regional, and World Special Olympics Games.
🤝
Promote social integration between Special Olympics athletes and mainstream athletes through unified team sports.
❤️
Encourage family involvement, strengthen community ties, and increase public awareness and respect for individuals with intellectual disabilities.

Sports & Key Programmes

Core Sport

Athletics

Year-round training in track and field events, fostering speed, strength, and endurance.

Core Sport

Badminton

Fostering racquet skills, agility, and hand-eye coordination through singles and doubles play.

Core Sport

Basketball

Building teamwork, ball-handling, and strategic court play in an energetic team environment.

Core Sport

Bocce

A precision ball sport emphasizing control, strategy, and inclusive participation across all skill levels.

Core Sport

Bowling

Targeting focus and technique in regular league play and competitive bowling events.

Core Sport

DanceSport

Combines rhythm, movement, and expressive dance performance for fitness and social connection.

Core Sport

Floorball

A fast-paced indoor hockey-style sport promoting quick reflexes and team strategy.

Core Sport

Football

Developing fundamental soccer skills, tactical cooperation, and camaraderie on the field.

Core Sport

Swimming

Comprehensive aquatics training building water safety, stamina, and competitive stroke techniques.

Flagship Programme

Athlete Leadership (ALP)

Empowers athletes with digital literacy (Canva, Google Tools) alongside NUS Youth Leaders, public speaking, and advocacy skills.

Flagship Programme

Young Athletes Programme

Structured movement and social skill development for children aged 2–7 with and without intellectual disabilities.

Flagship Programme

Healthy Athletes Programme

Free health screenings including MedFest, Special Smiles, Opening Eyes, Fit Feet, and FUNfitness.

Flagship Programme

Family Support Network (FSN)

"Family Supports Family" initiative fostering bonding outings, community interaction, and mutual support.

Flagship Programme

Motor Activity Training (MATP)

Tailored motor skill activities designed for athletes with severe physical or intellectual challenges.
