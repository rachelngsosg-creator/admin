🏅 About Us

Building inclusion through sport since 1983

Special Olympics Singapore (founded in 1983) is part of the world's largest sports movement for people with intellectual disabilities. Guided by a committed Board of Directors, we deliver year-round training and competitions across various Olympic-type sports, expand access to health and fitness, and grow leadership opportunities—so athletes can build fitness, demonstrate courage, experience joy, and be accepted and respected in their communities.
